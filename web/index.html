A/B Test Calculator

Plan a test, read its result and check its traffic split. Part of the ab-testing-toolkit project by Mohamed Moin Peer. Same formulas as the Python package (normal approximation, two-sided, alpha 0.05).

1 · How many visitors do I need?

Before launch: pick the smallest lift worth detecting.

2 · Is my result significant?

After the planned end date: enter visitors and conversions.

3 · Is the traffic split healthy?

Sample ratio mismatch check. A failure means the test is broken.

Examples are pre-filled with the synthetic "checkout redesign" and "unit price display" experiments from the project. Educational tool; results use the normal approximation and need reasonably large samples.
